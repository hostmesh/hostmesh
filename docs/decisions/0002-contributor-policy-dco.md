# 0002. Contributor policy: DCO sign-off

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-01
- Issue: Contributor policy: enforce DCO sign-off

## Context

The license is permissive and there is no plan to relicense or sell commercial licenses. A lightweight policy keeps the barrier for contributors low.

## Options considered

- **DCO sign-off**: contributors keep their copyright and certify their right to contribute. Light, but grants no relicensing rights.
- **CLA**: grants the project rights to relicense, at the cost of contributor friction.

## Decision

DCO sign-off on every commit, enforced by a pull request check. No CLA for now.

## Consequences

- Contributions are licensed under Apache-2.0.
- Relicensing future versions to something more restrictive would need contributors' consent.
- **Checkpoint:** before merging the first outside contribution, confirm DCO is still the right policy, or switch to a CLA.
